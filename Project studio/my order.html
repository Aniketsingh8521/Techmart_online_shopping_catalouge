Frequently Asked Questions

How is GST calculated on my Techmart.in bill?

All electronics on Techmart.in carry a standard 18% GST (9% CGST + 9% SGST), which is itemized clearly in your shopping cart and downloadable tax invoice.

What is the return & replacement policy?

We offer a 7-day replacement guarantee for damaged, defective, or wrong items delivered. Keep the original box and GST invoice handy.

How long do refunds take for cancelled orders?

Prepaid UPI, card, and net banking refunds are processed back to your original payment source within 2–4 business days.

How do I download my GST Tax Invoice?

Visit the My order page and click "View Tax Invoice" on any active or delivered order card.
