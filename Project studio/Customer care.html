Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders in metro cities arrive within 2–3 business days. Other locations across India typically take 4–6 business days.

What is your return and replacement policy?

We offer a 7-day free replacement policy for any damaged, defective, or incorrect tech item delivered.

Do laptops and GPUs come with brand warranty?

Yes. Every product sold on Techmart.in includes a GST invoice valid for official manufacturer warranty across India.

Can I cancel or change my order after placing it?

You can cancel or update your shipping address anytime before the item is dispatched from our warehouse.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Cash on Delivery is available on orders up to ₹25,000 across eligible PIN codes.
